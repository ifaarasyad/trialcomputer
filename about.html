About VIP Computer Care

VIP Computer Care provides on-site tech support, computer consulting, and personalized training in the West Hartford, CT and Hartford, CT areas. We help seniors, beginners, and anyone who needs extra support with their technology. Our goal is to make technology simple, stress-free, and easy to understand.

Meet Kevin

The person behind VIP Computer Care

Clients describe Kevin as patient, knowledgeable, and honest. He explains things in plain language, does only what needs to be done, and is easy to reach when something goes wrong.

Whether it is your very first computer or a tricky hardware repair, he works at your pace, at your location.

Who Does VIP Computer Care Serve?

VIP Computer Care serves the following customers, with a special focus on seniors and beginners.

Residential

Homeowners, renters, retirees, couples, and families who use technology at home. Whether it is your first computer, a new tablet from the grandkids, or Wi-Fi that keeps dropping, we come to you and explain everything at your pace.

Small Business

Local shops, home offices, independent professionals, and small teams that need dependable technology without hiring a full-time IT person. We help with computer setups, networks, printers, email, and data backup.

Non-Profit

Community groups, clubs, places of worship, and volunteer-run organizations. We keep your computers, network, and email running smoothly and help your staff and volunteers feel confident using them.
